# Cravr

**Hungry but can't decide?** Cravr asks a couple of quick questions, reads your mood, the time of day and what you usually order, then finds dishes on Swiggy that fit.

- Tap a suggestion or just type what you feel like.
- Pick a dish card to add it to your cart, choose add-ons, apply a coupon and check out.
- Say "show me more", "something cheaper" or "make it veg" at any point.

Built on Swiggy's MCP APIs.
